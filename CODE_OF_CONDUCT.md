# Code of conduct

Sellhawk follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good faith, give and take
feedback on the work rather than the person, and don't harass anyone, in
issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, make a private report through the
repository's Security tab. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may
remove comments, commits and issues, and may block anyone whose behaviour
doesn't fit this code, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines
describe.
